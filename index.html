Coding notes

  • ICD-10-CM describes the infant's diagnoses. CPT describes professional services and procedures. Hospital facility coders may separately use ICD-10-PCS for inpatient procedures.
  • Use the most specific code supported by the medical record. Do not select a diagnosis solely because it was considered in the differential.
  • Z38 is assigned only once, at birth. After a transfer, Z38 is not used at the receiving hospital.
  • When both are available, code birth weight before gestational age (tabular note under P07).
  • ICD-10-CM updates each October 1. The Oct 1, 2026 update has no changes to the perinatal chapter. CPT updates each January 1.
  • Procedures may be logged even when they are bundled into neonatal critical-care billing.

Practical distinctions

  • 99464 versus 99465: 99464 is requested attendance and stabilization at delivery. 99465 is actual resuscitation with positive-pressure ventilation and/or chest compressions.
  • Critical versus intensive care: 99468–99469 are neonatal critical care. 99477–99480 are intensive care for infants who are not currently critically ill.
  • Current versus birth weight: 99478–99480 use current weight. P07 birth-weight codes use birth weight.
  • After 28 days: a critically ill infant generally moves to the pediatric critical-care codes (99471–99472).
